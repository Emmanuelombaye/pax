## Typography

- **Instrument Serif** — Headings (H1–H4, display, editorial). Regular weight, slight negative tracking.
- **Work Sans** — Body & UI. Weights: **300, 400, 500, 600**.

## Recommended usage

- **Headings:** Instrument Serif, Ink on Sand. Reversed to Sand on Forest banners.
- **Body:** Work Sans 400, Ink on Sand. Line height 1.5–1.7. Avoid pure black.
- **Buttons:** Forest background, Sand text, Work Sans 500. Hover: Forest at 90% opacity.
- **Accents:** Terracotta for links, selection, focus rings, and small highlights — **never large areas**.
- **Surfaces:** Sand as the default canvas. Dune for soft cards and warm neutral blocks.
- **Logo on dark:** Sand or Dune on Forest / Ink. Clear space ≥ cap height around the mark.
