Typography
Instrument Serif — Headings
Used for H1–H4, display type, and editorial moments. Regular weight, slight negative tracking.
Work Sans — Body & UI
Weights in use: 300, 400, 500, 600. For paragraphs, labels, navigation, buttons.
Recommended Usage
Headings Instrument Serif, Ink (#1F1A16) on Sand. Reversed to Sand on Forest banners.
Body Work Sans 400, Ink on Sand. Line height 1.5–1.7. Avoid pure black.
Buttons Forest background, Sand text, Work Sans 500. Hover: Forest at 90% opacity.
Accents Terracotta for links, selection, focus rings, and small highlights — never large areas.
Surfaces Sand as the default canvas. Dune for soft cards and warm neutral blocks.
Logo on dark Use Sand or Dune on Forest / Ink. Maintain clear space ≥ cap height around the mark.
